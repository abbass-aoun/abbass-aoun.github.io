About

Software and hardware, with a pull toward medicine.

I am a Computer Engineering student at the Lebanese American University, graduating in December 2027. On the software side I do applied research on LLM privacy and safety in healthcare and build AI tools on retrieval and agent frameworks. On the hardware side I build robots and circuits that sense their surroundings and act on them.

I lead the IEEE Computer Engineering Club at LAU and tutor engineering and math courses. I am especially drawn to biomedical engineering: systems that read a signal from the body and make a reliable decision from it.
